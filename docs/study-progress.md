# Study Progress and Improvement Log

[← Documentation home](index.md) · [24-week roadmap](roadmap.md)

Use this page as a template for evidence-based study. Copy the tables into private
notes, an issue in your own fork, or another personal tracker. Do not commit personal,
employer, customer, account, incident, or interview-confidential information.

## Weekly study cycle

1. Select the roadmap focus and define one observable outcome.
2. Study the numbered module path and answer scenarios before reading model answers.
3. Complete the design exercise and timed mock interview.
4. Record score evidence and no more than three priority weaknesses.
5. Revisit the linked primary sources and practise the weak decisions.
6. Repeat the same mock or an equivalent prompt within seven days.
7. Advance when the result improves and no critical dimension remains weak.

## When the content needs improvement

A low score normally indicates a study gap. Open a repository issue when the page is
technically incorrect, unclear after prerequisite study, internally inconsistent,
outdated, or missing a scenario that exposes an important decision. Use the
[GitHub issue chooser](https://github.com/acloudmonk/cloud-devops-interview-preparation/issues/new/choose)
and include authoritative evidence without disclosing confidential information.
