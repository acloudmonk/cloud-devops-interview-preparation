# Full Architect Mock Interviews

Status: **Active pilot**

Use timed interview sets to practise discovery, structured design, failure
analysis, trade-offs, and executive communication.

## Available interviews

- [Cloud architecture and distributed systems](cloud-architecture.md) — a
  50-minute ticketing-platform interview with follow-up branches, a scorecard,
  and a repeatable improvement loop.
- [Linux production operations](linux-operations.md) — a 50-minute fleet and
  incident interview covering resource pressure, service lifecycle, recovery,
  secure access, and patching.
- [Cloud networking operations](networking-operations.md) — a 50-minute design
  and incident interview covering DNS, routes, policy, NAT, hybrid connectivity,
  and multi-cloud translation.
- [Git and source-control strategy](git-source-control.md) — a 50-minute workflow,
  governance, security, repository architecture, and recovery interview.

More interviews will be added only after the pilot format has been reviewed.
