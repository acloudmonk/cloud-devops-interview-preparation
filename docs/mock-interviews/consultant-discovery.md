# Timed Consultant Discovery Mock Interview

[← Consultant playbook](../interview-playbook/index.md)

Time: **50 minutes** · Score: **50 points** · Target: **40+**

## Candidate brief

A board wants a “cloud-first transformation” after repeated outages and rising cost.
The CIO wants speed, the CISO wants control, Finance disputes the baseline, operators
are overloaded, and a vendor-funded assessment already recommends multicloud. In 12
weeks, leadership expects an approved direction and first production pilot.

## Interview sequence

| Time | Prompt | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 0–6 | Frame decision, outcome, scope, ethics, and unknowns | 6 |
| 6–13 | Identify stakeholders, authority, incentives, and engagement | 7 |
| 13–21 | Run discovery across business, technology, and change | 8 |
| 21–28 | Define measurable requirements, evidence, and contradictions | 7 |
| 28–35 | Develop and compare credible options | 7 |
| 35–41 | Facilitate sponsor conflict and decision governance | 6 |
| 41–46 | Recommend roadmap, experiment, risks, and benefits measures | 5 |
| 46–50 | Deliver executive close and explicit decision ask | 4 |

## Injects

1. The CFO rejects every cost number.
2. Security says a pilot cannot use production-like data.
3. The vendor refuses raw benchmark evidence.
4. Operations says it cannot support another platform.

## Scorecard

Score each row up to its maximum. Any fabricated evidence, hidden conflict, unsafe
risk acceptance, or false consensus prevents a pass. Afterward record three gaps,
revisit the relevant worksheet/reference, and repeat with a different decision owner.
