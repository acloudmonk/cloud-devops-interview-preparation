# Cross-Domain Architecture Case Studies

Last reviewed: **2026-10-11**
Status: **Initial release**

These paper-and-whiteboard cases combine architecture, cloud, delivery, security,
reliability, operations, cost, migration, and stakeholder judgment. They require
no cloud account, application code, or infrastructure deployment.

!!! tip "Where to start"
    Read the **case-study method**, then use the common scorecard. Attempt each
    brief before reading its model direction. Follow the numbered path for a
    deliberate increase in ambiguity and cross-domain depth.

## Learning objectives

- discover outcomes, constraints, invariants, stakeholders, and missing evidence;
- compare credible options instead of jumping to a preferred cloud service;
- connect identity, data, networking, delivery, operations, resilience, and cost;
- reason through failure injects, recovery, migration, and organizational change;
- communicate a defensible recommendation to engineers and executives;
- identify assumptions and design the next experiment rather than bluff certainty.

## Practice modes

- **Solo, 60 minutes:** 10 discovery, 20 design, 15 failures, 10 presentation,
  5 scoring.
- **Paired, 75 minutes:** one interviewer reveals injects; one candidate leads.
- **Panel, 90 minutes:** assign product, security, finance, and operations roles.
- **Written review:** produce one diagram, decision record, risk register, and
  phased roadmap, then score the evidence rather than document length.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] I produced at least two options and made rejected trade-offs explicit.
- [ ] I quantified service objectives, recovery, capacity, and unit economics.
- [ ] I addressed security, delivery, operations, migration, and ownership.
- [ ] I completed the capstone under time pressure and scored at least 80/100.
- [ ] I can present a ten-minute executive recommendation without product dumping.
