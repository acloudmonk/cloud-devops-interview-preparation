# Consultant Rapid-Fire Revision

[← Advanced drills](scenario-drills.md) · [Next: Flashcards →](flashcards.md)

1. **First engagement question?** What decision must whom make by when and why?
2. **Outcome?** Measurable change in customer or business condition.
3. **Requirement?** Needed capability or quality.
4. **Constraint?** Boundary the option must respect.
5. **Preference?** Negotiable desired choice.
6. **Assumption?** Unverified belief temporarily used.
7. **Invariant?** Condition that must not be violated.
8. **Acceptance criterion?** Observable proof of completion.
9. **Fact quality?** Record source, date, confidence, and corroboration.
10. **Open question purpose?** Reveal context without prescribing an answer.
11. **Probe purpose?** Expose mechanism, evidence, impact, or exception.
12. **Quantification?** Convert adjective into measure and operating condition.
13. **Walk-through value?** Reveals real flow, ownership, and hidden dependency.
14. **Decision owner?** One person accountable for making the decision.
15. **Sponsor?** Provides authority, access, resources, and escalation.
16. **Affected stakeholder?** Bears impact even without formal authority.
17. **RACI danger?** Labels can hide the actual decision right.
18. **Stakeholder concern?** Expected loss, risk, or unmet outcome.
19. **Evidence question?** What proof would change your view?
20. **Workshop output?** Decisions, rationale, risks, actions, owners, dates.
21. **Pre-read purpose?** Reserve live time for conflict and decisions.
22. **Silent ideation benefit?** Reduces anchoring and airtime dominance.
23. **Parking lot rule?** Do not park a critical risk to manufacture closure.
24. **Silence equals consent?** Only if explicitly agreed beforehand.
25. **Conflict lens?** Interests, incentives, evidence, risk tolerance, time horizon.
26. **False consensus?** Agreement claimed despite unresolved dissent.
27. **Escalate when?** Authority, evidence, safety, or ethics blocks responsible work.
28. **Credible options?** Feasible alternatives, including improve or defer.
29. **Weighted scorecard?** Transparent aid to judgment, not automated truth.
30. **Sensitivity analysis?** Tests whether small assumption changes reverse choice.
31. **Baseline?** Current measured condition against which benefit is judged.
32. **Counterfactual?** What happens without the proposed change.
33. **Benefit owner?** Person accountable for realizing the outcome.
34. **Leading measure?** Early signal of progress.
35. **Lagging measure?** Later realized outcome.
36. **Reversible experiment?** Low-cost test that reduces decision uncertainty.
37. **Exit trigger?** Observable condition requiring review, stop, or change.
38. **Residual risk?** Risk remaining after chosen controls.
39. **Executive opening?** Decision, outcome, recommendation, and ask.
40. **Executive risk language?** Business impact, likelihood, exposure, owner.
41. **Technical narrative?** Context, requirements, architecture, failure, migration, proof.
42. **Confidence language?** Range, assumptions, source, and validation plan.
43. **Unknown answer?** Say so and explain how and when it will be resolved.
44. **Decision record value?** Preserves context, rationale, dissent, and triggers.
45. **Action without owner?** Not an action.
46. **Architecture adoption test?** Teams can fund, build, secure, operate, and change it.
47. **Consultant independence?** Honest evidence-based advice with disclosed conflicts.
48. **Discovery anti-pattern?** Asking questions that cannot affect the decision.
49. **Diagram anti-pattern?** Detail without a decision or audience purpose.
50. **Strong close?** Decision, trade-offs, proof, roadmap, owners, ask.
