# Consultant Discovery and Stakeholder Playbook

[← Documentation home](../index.md) · [Scenario answer framework](scenario-answer-framework.md)

Last reviewed: **2026-10-11**

Level: **Senior / Principal / Consultant**

This track teaches the work before architecture: framing the decision, discovering
business and technical realities, aligning stakeholders, exposing uncertainty, and
turning evidence into an executable recommendation.

!!! tip "Where to start"
    Begin with **1. Consultant mindset**, practise the exercises aloud, and finish
    with the scored mock. Use a real or case-study workload whenever possible.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] I can name the decision, owner, deadline, outcome, and acceptance evidence.
- [ ] I separate facts, assumptions, constraints, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- [ ] I map affected people, decision rights, incentives, objections, and communication.
- [ ] I convert vague requirements into measurable quality and business outcomes.
- [ ] I facilitate disagreement and document decisions without false consensus.
- [ ] I compare credible options, recommend one, and define validation and exit triggers.
- [ ] I completed the scenarios, drills, recall material, and scored mock.
