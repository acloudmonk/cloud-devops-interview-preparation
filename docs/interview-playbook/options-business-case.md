# Options, Value, and Recommendations

[← Conflict and ambiguity](conflict-ambiguity.md) · [Next: Communication →](executive-communication.md)

Create at least two credible options and include improve/current-state or defer when
honest. Eliminate infeasible options with evidence before scoring.

## Decision scorecard

Weight outcome value, time-to-value, customer impact, security/compliance, reliability,
operability, skills/change, cost, reversibility, dependency, and delivery risk. Scores
support judgment; they do not replace it. Show uncertainty and sensitivity—if a small
weight change reverses the result, the decision is fragile.

## Business case

Define baseline, investment, recurring cost, avoided cost/risk, benefit owner, leading
and lagging measures, time horizon, confidence range, dependencies, and counterfactual.
Do not present avoided catastrophe as guaranteed savings or false-precision ROI.

## Recommendation

State: “Recommend **option** because **evidence** best satisfies **criteria**, accepting
**trade-offs**. Validate **assumptions** through **experiment** by **date**. Stop, adapt,
or reconsider when **exit triggers** occur.”

Sequence irreversible commitments after cheaper learning. Include adoption, operating
model, security, data, migration, rollback, and benefits realization—not only build.
