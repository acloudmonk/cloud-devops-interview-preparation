# Stakeholders and Decision Rights

[← Discovery framework](discovery-framework.md) · [Next: Requirements and evidence →](requirements-evidence.md)

Stakeholders include decision owners, accountable executives, operators, builders,
security/risk, finance/procurement, legal/privacy, data owners, support, partners, and
people affected by the change—including users with little formal power.

## Map more than influence

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | What does this person need to improve or protect? |
| Authority | Decide, approve, advise, execute, operate, or receive information? |
| Evidence | What proof will change their view? |
| Incentive | What is rewarded, threatened, or made harder? |
| Concern | What loss or failure do they anticipate? |
| Engagement | Interview, workshop, review, approval, update, or escalation? |

Use a decision-rights model such as DACI or RACI only after clarifying the actual
decision. One named decision owner is preferable; committees advise and govern but can
hide accountability.

## Ethical inclusion

Power and impact are different. Seek operator and user evidence, not only executive
opinion. Provide asynchronous input for participants who cannot safely challenge a
room. Attribute decisions, protect sensitive comments, and do not weaponize stakeholder
maps.

## Communication cadence

Define sponsor sync, working-session cadence, decision checkpoints, risk escalation,
written decision log, and audience-specific updates. Record who was consulted, who was
not, and how that affects confidence.
