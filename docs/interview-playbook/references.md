## Suggested review loop

1. Apply one technique to a real or case-study decision.
2. Record what changed in evidence, alignment, or decision quality.
3. Ask a peer to challenge assumptions and stakeholder omissions.
4. Re-run the scenario with a different incentive or authority structure.
5. Update the template and retain only practices that improve outcomes.
