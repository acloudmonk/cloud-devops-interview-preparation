# Workshop Facilitation

[← Requirements and evidence](requirements-evidence.md) · [Next: Conflict and ambiguity →](conflict-ambiguity.md)

## Before

Publish purpose, required decision, pre-read, participants and roles, agenda, inputs,
working agreements, accessibility needs, and expected outputs. Interview key parties
before a high-conflict workshop. Do not use a workshop to discover basic facts that
could have been gathered asynchronously.

## During

1. Reconfirm decision and authority.
2. Display facts, assumptions, unknowns, and constraints separately.
3. Use silent idea generation before discussion to reduce anchoring.
4. Time-box divergence, then evaluate against agreed criteria.
5. Invite dissent and affected voices; prevent title or airtime from deciding.
6. Maintain visible decisions, risks, parking lot, actions, owners, and dates.
7. Summarize and request correction before closing.

## After

Within the agreed time, distribute outcomes, not a transcript: decision, rationale,
options rejected, assumptions, unresolved risks, actions, owner, due date, evidence,
and next checkpoint. Silence is not approval unless that rule was agreed in advance.

## Failure modes

Avoid solutioning before framing, agenda overload, performative voting, anonymous
ownership, revisiting settled decisions without new evidence, and parking critical
risks merely because time expired.
