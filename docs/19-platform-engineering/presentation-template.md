# Ten-Minute Platform Architecture Review

[← Advanced platform drills](scenario-drills.md) · [Next: Rapid-fire revision →](rapid-fire.md)

## Minute 0–1 — Customers and problem

State personas, journey baseline, business impact, repeated friction, non-goals,
and why a shared product is justified.

## Minute 1–3 — Product and responsibility

Describe thin viable platform, golden paths, product ownership, consumer duties,
support tiers, decision rights, roadmap, and escape hatches.

## Minute 3–5 — Architecture and lifecycle

Walk through interfaces, catalog, orchestration, control, resources, evidence,
desired state, identity, authority, status, update, and deletion.

## Minute 5–7 — Security and delivery

Explain isolation, workload identity, build trust, provenance, policy, promotion,
progressive change, audit, and exception governance.

## Minute 7–9 — Operation and adoption

Cover journey SLOs, dependencies, failure behavior, on-call, recovery, versions,
pilot, migration, feedback, deprecation, and multi-cloud boundaries.

## Minute 9–10 — Value and decision

Connect flow, quality, reliability, experience, adoption, and unit economics. Name
the next reversible investment, assumptions, residual risks, and stop criteria.
