# Discovery, Portfolio, and Business Case

[← Decision model](concepts.md) · [Next: Strategies and target state →](strategies-target-state.md)

## Build a usable inventory

Combine CMDB, cloud and virtualization exports, network flows, DNS, identity, source
repositories, pipelines, monitoring, financial records, contracts, interviews, and
business-process maps. For every workload record owner, criticality, users, lifecycle,
technology, environments, capacity, data classification, availability, recovery,
cost, license, support, change windows, and known dependencies.

Inventory is evidence with confidence, not a perfect spreadsheet. Mark source,
collection date, confidence, gaps, and accountable validator. Resolve high-impact
unknowns first and continue refining through waves.

## Discover dependencies in several ways

- observed network and process communication;
- application configuration, APIs, queues, files, DNS, certificates, and batch jobs;
- database links, replication, reporting extracts, and identity dependencies;
- business journeys, calendars, external partners, and shared operations;
- interviews and cutover rehearsals.

No method is complete. Reconcile contradictory evidence and identify hidden shared
services, circular dependencies, latency sensitivity, unsupported protocols, and
dependencies that cannot move together.

## Segment and rationalize

Group by business capability, dependency, criticality, compliance, lifecycle,
complexity, technology, and change appetite. Identify obvious retire/retain candidates,
quick learning waves, constraint-removal work, and workloads requiring deeper analysis.
Do not use application age alone as a modernization score.

## Build the business case

Compare realistic scenarios: remain, relocate, rehost, replatform, repurchase, or
modernize. Include migration delivery, dual running, network, licenses, people,
training, support, cloud consumption, commitments, risk, downtime, decommissioning,
contract exit, and contingency. Show cash flow, sensitivity, confidence, time to value,
and nonfinancial benefits rather than an unsupported percentage saving.

## Discovery exit evidence

A workload is ready for disposition when ownership, boundaries, critical dependencies,
data/control needs, lifecycle, baseline demand/cost, target constraints, validation
owner, and major unknowns are explicit. Unknowns may remain, but each needs an owner,
deadline, and treatment in wave risk.
