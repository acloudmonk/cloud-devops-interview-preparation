# Ten-Minute Migration and Modernization Review

[← Advanced drills](scenario-drills.md) · [Next: Rapid-fire revision →](rapid-fire.md)

Use one minute per section. Lead with decisions and evidence; keep tool names secondary.

## 1. Outcome and forcing event

State why change is required, deadline, scope, success measures, constraints, and the
cost or risk of inaction.

## 2. Portfolio evidence

Show workload boundaries, owners, criticality, lifecycle, dependency confidence, data,
cost, and the most important unknowns.

## 3. Disposition and target

Explain strategy criteria, example decisions, minimum landing state, later
modernization, and evidence that could change the selection.

## 4. Foundation readiness

Cover organization, identity, network, security, logging, recovery, automation,
capacity, cost, support, and the representative path used to prove them.

## 5. Waves and factory

Explain dependency grouping, first-wave learning, entry/exit gates, flow metrics,
governance, decision rights, and how lessons change later waves.

## 6. Transition architecture

Name authority during coexistence, synchronization, temporary connectivity, identity,
routing, data validation, and removal of transitional components.

## 7. Cutover and recovery

Describe rehearsal, freeze/fencing, go/no-go, monitoring, abort thresholds, rollback
deadline, post-cutover writes, reconciliation, and communications.

## 8. Acceptance and security

Show functional and nonfunctional tests, control evidence, RTO/RPO proof, service
ownership, runbooks, on-call, hypercare, exceptions, and residual risk.

## 9. Value and closure

Show unit economics, benefits, optimization, modernization, skills, legacy shutdown,
records, licenses, contracts, and separate completion states.

## 10. Recommendation

Restate the next decision, first deliverable, top three risks, accountable owners,
evidence needed, and when leadership should stop or change course.
