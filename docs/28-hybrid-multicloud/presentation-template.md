# Ten-Minute Hybrid/Multi-Cloud Strategy Review

[← Module overview](index.md) · [Design exercise](design-exercise.md)

1. **0–1 — Outcome:** business drivers, users, constraints, scope, and why multiple environments.
2. **1–2 — Placement:** workload/data matrix, pattern, temporary versus steady state, rejected simplification.
3. **2–3 — Boundaries:** topology, IP/routing/DNS, trust, data authority, and failure domains.
4. **3–4 — Connectivity:** path diversity, encryption, inspection, capacity, latency, and failover.
5. **4–5 — Identity/security:** federation, native authorization, keys, delivery trust, emergency recovery.
6. **5–6 — Data/platform:** consistency, integration, portability tier, artifacts, provider-native features.
7. **6–7 — Operations:** inventory, SLOs, telemetry, incident command, support, drift, skills.
8. **7–8 — Resilience:** degraded/disconnected modes, correlated failures, recovery, failback, exit.
9. **8–9 — Economics/governance:** TCO, egress, duplication, contracts, authority, exceptions, retirement.
10. **9–10 — Recommendation:** roadmap, evidence, top risks, investment, decisions, residual exposure.

Close with: “For **[business driver]**, place **[components/data]** in **[locations]**
using **[pattern]**. We will prove **[outcomes]** through **[tests/metrics]**; leadership
must fund or accept **[complexity/residual risk]**, and we will simplify when **[trigger]**.”
