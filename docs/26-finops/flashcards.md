# Active-Recall Flashcards

[← Module overview](index.md) · [Rapid-fire revision](rapid-fire.md)

Use spaced repetition. Say the answer before expanding each card.

1. **FinOps purpose?** Maximize business value of technology through collaboration, data, and accountability.
2. **Core decision loop?** Scope → data → allocation/economics → decision → action → realized value → iterate.
3. **Core personas?** Engineering, FinOps practitioner, finance, leadership, procurement, and product.
4. **Inform / Optimize / Operate?** See and align / improve usage and rate / govern and repeat.
5. **Allocation hierarchy?** Organization boundary, account/project, metadata, business rules, measured drivers, shared policy.
6. **Showback / chargeback?** Visibility without / with financial transfer.
7. **Unit economics formula?** Defined cost numerator divided by meaningful demand/value denominator, with quality guardrails.
8. **Cost-basis check?** Scope, time, currency, net/amortized/unblended treatment, tax, credit, support, and marketplace.
9. **Data-quality dimensions?** Freshness, completeness, schema, duplicates, allocation, adjustments, and invoice reconciliation.
10. **Persona-specific reporting?** Same governed data, different decisions and appropriate access.
11. **Forecast building blocks?** Demand, baseline, growth, events, initiatives, architecture, rate, optimization, and uncertainty.
12. **Variance dimensions?** Price, quantity, mix, timing, allocation, and one-time adjustment.
13. **Anomaly response?** Validate → attribute → investigate → contain safely → communicate → verify → prevent.
14. **Usage optimization?** Remove or reshape consumption while maintaining quality and risk constraints.
15. **Rate optimization?** Reduce price for appropriate demand through commitments, reservations, contracts, or licenses.
16. **Realized-savings test?** Post-change cost adjusted for demand, rate, fees, implementation, and quality.
17. **Commitment decision?** Stable demand, scenarios, coverage, utilization, break-even, flexibility, owner, and expiry.
18. **Kubernetes cost layers?** Cloud resources, platform services, workload use, idle/headroom, and business attribution.
19. **Shared-cost rule?** Use measurable causation where decision value exceeds complexity; disclose estimates and overhead.
20. **Platform value?** Cost plus adoption, productivity, reliability, security, support, and duplication removed.
21. **Governance model?** Central enablement with decentralized workload ownership and explicit decision rights.
22. **Policy automation safety?** Scope, authorization, dry run, quality guardrail, rollback, audit, and exception.
23. **FOCUS value and limit?** Common schema reduces normalization; provider semantics still require native detail.
24. **Sustainability trade-off?** Measure carbon and cost separately with latency, resilience, sovereignty, and migration constraints.
25. **Architect answer shape?** Outcome → data/basis → driver → choice → guardrail → owner → realized value/risk.
