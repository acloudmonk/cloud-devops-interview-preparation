# Enterprise Portfolio Design Exercise

[← Capability matrices](capability-matrices.md) · [Next: Scenarios →](scenarios.md)

## Brief

A regulated global company has AWS-native commerce, Azure-centered workforce and data
teams, and an acquired GCP analytics estate. Leaders demand “cloud agnosticism,”
cross-cloud recovery, unified security, and 20% savings. The team has inconsistent
identity, overlapping networks, duplicated pipelines, three telemetry stacks, large
egress bills, and no tested failover.

## Produce

1. Clarified business drivers, journeys, SLOs, RTO/RPO, data classes, and constraints.
2. A placement scorecard and target pattern for each workload.
3. Native hierarchy/landing-zone design with shared governance intent.
4. Workforce/workload federation, authorization, keys, and emergency access.
5. Network, DNS, ingress/egress, intercloud, and private-service architecture.
6. Runtime, data, integration, delivery, and observability choices with semantic gaps.
7. Recovery authority, replication, fencing, capacity, reconciliation, and exercises.
8. Total cost, quotas, risks, evidence plan, roadmap, and exit triggers.

## Evaluation

Score 0–2 for discovery, mapping accuracy, placement, governance, identity/security,
network, data/integration, reliability, operations, economics, and communication.
**18/22** is strong; unsafe identity or data-integrity design cannot pass.
