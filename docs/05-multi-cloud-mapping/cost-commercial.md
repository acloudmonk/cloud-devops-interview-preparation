# Cost, Contracts, Capacity, and Sustainability

[← Operations](operations.md) · [Next: Portability and placement →](portability-placement.md)

Normalize provider billing exports into provider, account/subscription/project, owner,
environment, service, SKU, region, label/tag, and business unit. Preserve the native
invoice and usage detail for reconciliation.

## Total portfolio cost

Include compute, storage operations, observability, security tooling, support, network
circuits, inter-region/intercloud egress, data replication, duplicate licenses,
commitment waste, engineering skills, compliance evidence, and recovery exercises.

Savings Plans/reservations, Azure reservations/savings plans, and Google Cloud committed
use discounts have different scopes and risks. Buy commitments after rightsizing and
stable-demand evidence; do not treat discount percentage as architecture quality.

Quota and capacity are provider-, account-, project-, region-, zone-, service-, and SKU-
specific. Forecast failure demand separately and test what happens when capacity or a
quota increase is unavailable.

Use business unit economics such as cost per order or tenant. For sustainability,
reduce idle duplicate estates, improve utilization, lifecycle data, and limit needless
transfer while balancing carbon signals with residency, latency, and reliability.
