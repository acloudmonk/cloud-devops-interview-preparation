## Suggested review loop

1. Write journeys, qualities, demand, data, and constraints.
2. Draw hierarchy, location, network, trust, ownership, and cost boundaries.
3. Compare two runtime, data, integration, and ingress options.
4. Inject identity, zone, region, quota, key, DNS, and data failures.
5. Define isolated restore and business-integrity proof.
6. Answer scenarios aloud, score the mock, and revisit weak primary sources.
