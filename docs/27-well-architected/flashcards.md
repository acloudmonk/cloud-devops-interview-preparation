# Active-Recall Flashcards

[← Module overview](index.md) · [Rapid-fire revision](rapid-fire.md)

Use spaced repetition. Say the answer before expanding each card.

1. **Review purpose?** Shared evidence, material risks, conscious trade-offs, and validated improvement.
2. **Workload boundary?** Components and operations delivering one business outcome, across administrative/provider boundaries if needed.
3. **Review model?** Context → objectives → architecture → evidence → risk → decision → learning.
4. **Evidence confidence?** Source, owner, environment, date, scope, and strength from claim to representative test.
5. **AWS pillars?** Operations, security, reliability, performance, cost, sustainability.
6. **Azure pillars?** Reliability, security, cost, operations, performance.
7. **Google Cloud pillars?** Operations, security/privacy/compliance, reliability, cost, performance, sustainability.
8. **Lens or perspective?** Additional workload/industry/technology guidance applied across core quality concerns.
9. **Finding anatomy?** Observation, evidence, uncertainty, scenario, consequence, controls, priority, options, owner, validation.
10. **Risk severity check?** Business consequence and exposure calibrated to workload context, not provider label alone.
11. **Exception anatomy?** Unmet expectation, rationale, compensation, residual risk, acceptor, expiry, trigger.
12. **Trade-off process?** Outcome, attributes, constraints, options, cross-pillar effects, decision, evidence, revisit.
13. **ADR purpose?** Preserve why a decision was made, its consequences, and when to reconsider.
14. **Safe facilitation?** Challenge claims and systems with evidence while avoiding blame and hidden commercial influence.
15. **Conflicting evidence?** Record both scopes/times, name uncertainty, assign validation, escalate only as risk requires.
16. **Improvement groups?** Containment, investigation, quick action, foundation, strategic change, and accepted risk.
17. **Closure evidence?** Tested or measured risk reduction plus updated design and authorized residual risk.
18. **Continuous trigger?** New workload, major change, incident, threat, regulation, demand, exception expiry, or milestone.
19. **Portfolio learning?** Repeated findings become platform, policy, training, ownership, or funding improvements.
20. **Automation boundary?** Gather facts and enforce approved policy; humans decide applicability, trade-off, and acceptance.
21. **Misleading metric?** Raw score, review count, answer completion, or ticket closure without outcome context.
22. **Cross-cloud method?** Common business/quality context, provider-specific evidence, consolidated risks, retained differences.
23. **Executive report?** Top risks, confidence, choices, investment, owners, timeline, and residual decisions.
24. **Review stop condition?** No accountable owner, undefined scope, or evidence restrictions that make conclusions misleading.
25. **Architect answer shape?** Outcome/scope → evidence → risk → options/trade-off → authority → roadmap → validation.
