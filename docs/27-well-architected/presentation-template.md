# Ten-Minute Executive Architecture Review

[← Module overview](index.md) · [Design exercise](design-exercise.md)

Use this structure for an executive readout. Keep framework detail available in
the appendix; the presentation should support decisions.

## Minute 0–1 — Purpose and workload

State business outcome, critical users/journeys, lifecycle, owners, providers,
review trigger, included scope, exclusions, and framework/lenses used.

## Minute 1–2 — Objectives and priorities

Summarize security/privacy obligations, SLOs, recovery, performance, cost/value,
sustainability goals, delivery needs, and which attributes take priority.

## Minute 2–3 — Evidence and confidence

Explain participants, reviewed artifacts, tests, telemetry, incidents, provider
tools, evidence age, material gaps, and confidence limits.

## Minute 3–5 — Top risks

For each top risk state factual condition, scenario, business consequence, existing
control, likelihood/exposure, confidence, and urgency. Consolidate cross-pillar duplicates.

## Minute 5–6 — Trade-offs and decisions

Present viable options and effects across security, reliability, operations,
performance, cost, sustainability, delivery, and organizational capability.

## Minute 6–7 — Improvement roadmap

Separate containment, quick improvements, foundations, strategic changes, and
investigations. Name owners, dependencies, effort range, target, and validation.

## Minute 7–8 — Exceptions and residual risk

List accepted/deferred items, compensating controls, accountable acceptor, expiry,
reassessment trigger, and unresolved decisions.

## Minute 8–9 — Continuous assurance

Describe milestones, delivery/change triggers, automated evidence, specialist tests,
outcome metrics, portfolio themes, and next review date.

## Minute 9–10 — Recommendation

Summarize the three highest-value decisions, investment needed, consequence of delay,
confidence, residual exposure, and explicit executive actions.

## Closing sentence pattern

“For **[workload/outcome]**, evidence shows **[condition]** creates **[business risk]**.
I recommend **[decision and roadmap]**, owned by **[roles]**, validated through
**[evidence]**; leadership must decide **[trade-off/residual risk]** by **[date]**.”
