# Advanced Review-Room Drills

[← Module overview](index.md) · [Scenario questions](scenarios.md)

Use five minutes per drill. Establish review scope, facts, confidence, affected
outcome, stakeholder dynamics, safe next action, decision owner, and validation.

## Drill 1 — Diagram and production disagree

The approved diagram shows private service traffic, but telemetry indicates an
internet path. Decide how to validate, protect sensitive evidence, manage active
risk, update artifacts, and word the finding without blaming the architect.

## Drill 2 — No business owner attends

Engineers know implementation but cannot approve criticality, cost, or risk. Narrow
the technical discovery, label missing decisions, avoid false acceptance, and plan
a short follow-up with the accountable owner.

## Drill 3 — “Not applicable” hides legacy debt

Several framework questions are marked not applicable because remediation is hard.
Reassess applicability, record current condition and constraint, express business
risk, compare compensating controls, and require time-bound acceptance.

## Drill 4 — Evidence contains secrets

A participant uploads credentials and sensitive network details to the review record.
Restrict access, remove exposure through approved procedures, rotate credentials,
preserve necessary audit facts, and redesign evidence handling.

## Drill 5 — Provider guidance changed

A prior decision followed earlier official guidance that has since evolved. Determine
whether current risk changes, preserve historical rationale, evaluate migration cost,
and schedule proportionate remediation rather than declaring immediate noncompliance.

## Drill 6 — A platform issue affects 40 workloads

Every review finds the same identity and observability gaps. Aggregate the risk,
fund platform controls, define interim workload mitigations, assign central and local
owners, and measure system-level removal of recurring findings.

## Drill 7 — Sustainability data is uncertain

Teams claim a Region move will reduce emissions but evidence is aggregated and latency
will rise. State data confidence, compare cost/performance/reliability/sovereignty,
consider reversible experiments, and avoid unsupported precision.

## Drill 8 — Reviewer and team disagree on severity

Separate factual condition from risk scoring. Align threat/failure scenario, impact,
exposure, controls, confidence, and risk model. Record dissent and route the decision
to the authorized owner; do not change severity merely to end debate.

## Drill 9 — Acquisition creates three architectures

The business wants one review and immediate standardization. Define workload boundaries,
identify common journeys and platform controls, review material differences, and sequence
standardization by risk and value rather than aesthetic consistency.

## Drill 10 — Remediation causes a regression

A security hardening change raises latency beyond the product SLO. Stabilize service,
preserve the security objective, reassess options and test design, update the finding
and ADR, and add cross-pillar validation to closure criteria.

## Self-review prompts

- Did I distinguish observation, uncertainty, risk, recommendation, and decision?
- Did I use business impact rather than framework authority as the reason?
- Did I protect sensitive evidence and psychological safety?
- Did I name the authorized owner and capture dissent or constraints?
- Did I validate outcomes across pillars rather than close a ticket?
- Did I improve a platform or policy when the finding was systemic?
